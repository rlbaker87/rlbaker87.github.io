Compliance Training

Annual Compliance Training Redesign

A full redesign of an enterprise compliance training program, converting passive, text-heavy content into performance-based, scenario-driven eLearning for a workforce spanning two states.

30K+ Team Members at Launch
3 Differentiated Audience Tracks
4-6 hrs Estimated Build Time Per Module
June 2026 Scheduled Launch

The Challenge

The Annual Compliance Training consisted of definition-heavy content that asked team members to read and recall policy rather than practice applying it. The program serves a large, diverse workforce spanning clinical and non-clinical roles across two states, but scenarios and examples were often clinical in framing and inaccessible to the majority of learners. Content needed to be restructured so it was relevant, decision-focused, and applicable to every role in the organization.

Solution

Every module was rebuilt around a standardized four-step instructional model applied consistently across the program.

Step 1

Context

Frame the topic in terms of real workplace situations, not policy definitions

Step 2

Practice

Sorting activities and scenario-based knowledge checks replace passive reading

Step 3

Feedback

Immediate, specific feedback tied to the scenario rather than generic correct/incorrect

Step 4

Performance Support

Reference resources embedded at point of need for post-training application

  • Developed a six-prompt Microsoft Copilot Enterprise workflow that transforms existing course design documents into complete Rise 360-ready module drafts, reducing per-module build time to an estimated 4 to 6 hours for a four-module course.
  • Built a custom Copilot declarative agent to automate HTML block generation for team members, using a structured five-step intake sequence and distributable via ZIP export.
  • Created differentiated versions for two additional audiences: an executive briefing format for VP-level leaders using scenario-based action questions and a dashboard structure in place of a scored quiz, and a tailored track for volunteers.
  • Established an ACT Style Guide and eLearning Design Standards document codifying writing rules, Rise 360 theme configuration, feedback standards, accessibility requirements, and module time targets.
AI-Assisted Authoring

The Copilot prompt series is a six-step workflow that takes an existing design document and produces a structured Rise 360 module draft, including scenario text, interaction copy, and feedback language. Documented session handoff procedures prevent context loss between sessions, making the workflow repeatable across the full team rather than dependent on a single author.

Differentiated Audience Tracks

Core Workforce

Scenario-based modules with sorting activities, knowledge checks, and embedded performance support. Applicable across all clinical and non-clinical roles.

Executive Track

Briefing format for VP-level leaders. Scenario-based action questions and a dashboard structure replace traditional scored assessments.

Volunteer Track

Tailored version scoped to the specific compliance requirements and workplace contexts relevant to volunteer team members.
