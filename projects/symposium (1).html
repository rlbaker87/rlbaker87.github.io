Event Design / Leadership Development

2025 Leadership Symposium

A full-day leadership development event for 400+ directors and above, planned and executed by a cross-functional team of seven project groups across a compressed planning cycle.

The Challenge

The annual Leadership Symposium needed to move beyond a traditional all-hands format and deliver a genuinely useful development experience for senior leaders across a large, complex health system. The planning team operated on a compressed timeline with a June kickoff, which created cascading pressure across session design, communications, and logistics. Coordinating seven functional teams, 35+ presenters, and 20 concurrent sessions required a level of operational structure the event had not previously had in place.

Event Structure

The event was built around three strategic themes, with 20 concurrent sessions distributed across three time blocks to give attendees meaningful choice in their development experience.

  • Built reusable project templates, communication resources, and a structured Microsoft Teams channel system to coordinate across all seven teams throughout the planning cycle.
  • Conducted personalized presenter outreach to strengthen session quality and increase participation prestige across 35+ speakers.
  • Managed registration and attendee flow for 400+ directors and above, adapting in real time to venue capacity considerations at the Embassy Suites location.
  • Facilitated a comprehensive post-event SWOT analysis with 15 to 18 team leaders representing all functional areas, producing an executive summary with strategic recommendations for future iterations.
Post-Event Analysis

The post-event SWOT produced five strategic priority areas for 2026 planning, including earlier kickoff timelines, technology infrastructure improvements, and team structure refinements. The analysis was structured to go beyond satisfaction scores and surface operational insights the planning team could act on.
