The Challenge

There was nothing in the queue when Claude Design launched. Rather than run a generic test, the challenge became a real question: can a defensible, content-validated, interactive learning artifact be built from scratch using only AI tools, publicly available curriculum standards, and a documented workflow?

The content domain needed to be authentic enough to matter. A childhood memory of watching construction truck videos with my son pointed toward heavy equipment operators, a workforce that is underserved by eLearning and whose safety training has real consequences. That was worth building.

The Workflow

Full ideation, validation, and build executed across four tools in a deliberate sequence, each chosen for what it does best.

1

Game mechanics, field layout, traffic escalation, question bank structure, checkpoint logic, scoring, win and game over states
2
Perplexity / Gemini / ChatGPT
Content Validation

Validated content synthesized into a structured Claude Design prompt covering visuals, game logic, question bank, and bilingual requirements

Full interactive prototype built through conversation. Instructions screen, three difficulty levels, question cards, coaching tips, win and game over screens, English and Spanish toggle.

Solution

  • Completed all ideation and text-based storyboarding in Claude chat before opening any build tool. Game mechanics, field layout, traffic escalation across three difficulty levels, a checkpoint question system, near miss consequences, and scoring logic were all defined in conversation first.
  • Developed a question bank grounded in construction safety practice, then ran verification prompts through Perplexity, Gemini, and ChatGPT to cross-reference content against NCCER Heavy Equipment Operations curriculum and OSHA construction standards.
  • Conducted a full terminology review against authentic heavy civil construction jobsite language, confirming or correcting eleven terms including spotter, haul road, walk-around, blind spot, near miss, and swing radius for accuracy with a dam construction site audience.
  • Used Claude chat to engineer a structured handoff prompt for Claude Design, translating all validated content and game logic specs into a format the tool could build from directly.
  • Executed the full prototype build in Claude Design through iterative conversation and inline editing, producing a playable HTML artifact with three escalating levels, bilingual English and Spanish support, and a complete question and coaching system.
  • Documented the workflow publicly on LinkedIn, including an honest account of token limit interruptions that left the Spanish version incomplete mid-build.

Content Validation

Without SME access, external AI validation against published standards was the quality gate. Two items came back flagged and were corrected before the build.

Question Flagged: Blind Spot Direction

Original question assumed the passenger side is always the larger blind spot on a bulldozer. Perplexity flagged this as too absolute. Blind spots vary by machine model, attachments, and terrain. Replaced with a question asking what operators should assume about blind spots in general.

Corrected before build

Question Flagged: Eye Contact Protocol

Original framing implied eye contact alone is the safety standard for ground worker approach. OSHA and NCCER guidance is broader. Both parties must acknowledge each other and the operator must confirm it is safe. Rewritten to reflect the full protocol.

Corrected before build

Terminology: 11 Terms Reviewed

Walk-around, spotter, haul road, near miss, cab, blade, and ground conditions confirmed as accurate. Blind side updated to blind spot. Eye contact and signal updated to make eye contact and wait for acknowledgment. Yellow iron flagged as informal and used as flavor only.

Language locked before build

Five Terms Added from Field Review

Cut and fill, ripper, bench, berm, and lift identified as terms a bulldozer operator on a dam site would commonly use. Incorporated into coaching tips and question framing where appropriate to add site authenticity.

Added to question context

Honest Reflection on Claude Design

The output quality is strong, and watching it build a functional interactive prototype in real time is genuinely impressive. It took very little back and forth to make visual and mechanical adjustments.

Rushing through the design phase of the process had a visible impact. Elements that were not storyboarded explicitly required more iteration inside the tool. The workflow investment up front in Claude chat paid off. The more detailed the prompt, the cleaner the first output.

Worth Knowing Before You Plan Around It

Token limits will stop you mid-build. The Spanish version of this game was not finished when the weekly cap was hit. That is a meaningful constraint for any course of real scope. Branching logic, LMS integration, and accessibility compliance are also not there yet, though workarounds are beginning to surface. This is a tool that earns a place in a deliberate workflow. It does not replace the workflow.
